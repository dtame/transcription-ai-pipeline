# Concept 1 — The door already open

A quiet domestic threshold, already open, with light already in the room. The picture argues access rather than achievement.

## Themes from the canonical book

- The Life You Already Inherited
- Resurrection life, union with God, and the practice of spiritual authority
- Access, Not Achievement
- The Power Is Already Inside
- Operating What You Have

- #E7D7C1 warm plaster

## Elements

- an interior door standing open
- a small oil lamp already lit on a plain table
- an even plaster wall

## Composition

Vertical 2:3. The upper third is an even plaster field reserved for the title. The doorway and lamp occupy the lower two-thirds, slightly left of center.

## Title space

Upper third, low detail, #E7D7C1, dark type to be added by the renderer.

## Contrast

## Risks

- Wood grain can resemble letters.
