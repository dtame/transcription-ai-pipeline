# Set Your Mind Above

## Fixing the Affections

Get into your fellowship. Enter into it deliberately, each night, and speak to the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit who dwell within you. Say to him: Father, I know you are inside of me. Jesus, I know you are here. I am your temple. Let our fellowship be this night. Let my fellowship be this night. And set your mind there.

This is what Colossians chapter 3 commands: set your affections on the things above. That does not mean you will stop thinking about the things of the world altogether. You will think about them — you will think about them — there is simply a way to think about them, a way that keeps them from ruling your attention.

When you practice this, your mind will shake at first. You will have a lot of distractions wanting to pull you away. Things will try to penetrate that sphere you have set your focus in. But keep your mind there. By tomorrow, you will fly into this place — sustained awareness comes not immediately but through persistence against the distraction.

I am giving you a duty here, not an abstraction. As I said earlier, I don't want us to do what we cannot practice. I have been giving you words — kingdom words. Now I am giving you how it is done. It doesn't fail. It has never failed. It is constant.

Philippians chapter 3 verse 20 tells us we are citizens of heaven. But more important than that citizenship is this: he is sitting, and your heart, your spirit, is his seat. The practice, then, is not an escape to some faraway heaven — it is recognizing that heaven's reality already dwells within you.

Close your eyes and say it: I see Jesus. I see Jesus on the throne of my heart. I see Jesus. You are dead — your natural self is hidden in Christ.

## Selecting Your Thoughts

Look at Philippians chapter 4 verse 8. It tells us how to walk there — not only in the quiet of the night, but as you walk your walk going home in the taxi, in the car, in the bus, or flying in the airplane.

Finally, brethren: all that is true, all that is honorable, all that is just, all that is pure, all that is wonderful, everything that is worthy of praise — dwell on these things.

There are things that will be happening around you. Are they pure things? Are they excellent things? Are they things worthy of praise? You have a great deal of selection — many things you can think about. Choose them deliberately, filtering for what is pure, what is excellent, what is praiseworthy.

The battlefield of life is in the mind. It is in the thought. It is not won or lost by outward circumstance but by what you allow your thinking to dwell on.

## Complete in the Head

Remember what we read in Colossians 2. Verse 9 says all of God's fullness dwells in him — the head. Verse 10 says you are complete in him. You are completely in him. So you are completing him; so you are completing him.

The principalities and powers you are wrestling with are not flesh and blood. You wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities and powers, against spiritual wickedness in the heavenly places.

Do you think the head will function without the body? The head is so powerful, and yet his body is riddled with sickness, with all kinds of infirmity, all kinds of poverty and penury. Something is wrong with our doctrine.
