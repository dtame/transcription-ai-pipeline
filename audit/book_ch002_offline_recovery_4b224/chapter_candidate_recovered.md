# Flesh and Bone: The Resurrection You Inherit

## Spirits Have No Flesh and Bone

Some of our best messages have actually prepared people to die well rather than teaching them to inherit resurrection life. We quote that flesh and blood cannot inherit the kingdom of God, but we stop there. People don't read the whole thing. Because the same passage that says flesh and blood cannot inherit also tells us that flesh and bone can inherit it.

When Jesus rose, his resurrection was physical, not merely spiritual. He called Thomas and showed him that spirits do not have flesh and bone. He did not simply disappear into heaven as a spirit would vanish. He ascended, bodily, in flesh and bone.

Before the throne of grace there is a man who is sitting there. His name is Jesus. This is not a purely spiritual or symbolic presence — it is a man, in flesh and bone, seated at the throne.

I was hearing somebody the other day teach that this resurrection life, this inheritance, is merely a 'positional status' — that you can never actually have that experience yourself. I say plainly: that teaching is antichrist, and it is heresy.

## From Sons of Men to Sons of God

Jesus did not come merely so that we would live in the spirit. He came so that sons of men would become sons of God. That is what he came for.

He came to restore what the Father had intended from the beginning.

Adam was son of God. But when he made the mistake he made, he began to father children after his own fallen image rather than after God's image. From that point, as Genesis chapter 5 records, humanity became sons of men rather than sons of God.

Time arrested Adam when he sinned. He broke the chain of immortality; he broke the chain of eternity — a chain he had carried even in the moment of his sin. The exact shape of this teaching remains dense and was not fully drawn out, but the claim stands: something of eternity was broken by that fall.

That is what Jesus did: he destroyed death and brought life and immortality to light, making it manifest, restoring access back to the tree of life.

## The Silence of Adam, the Prayer of Christ

Eve was deceived. At that moment she did not have all her senses about her; her act was not a deliberate, knowing transgression the way a willful sin would be.

Man became responsible because man was the womb — it is from the man that the woman was taken. Because of that, his role immediately became to cover the woman; he became the first respondent, the first one responsible before God.

The man was there, present at the temptation. He said, 'You can't do this' — but he did not go further than that. He could have stayed there and cried out, interceding for her before God. And God would have forgiven the wife, had Adam interceded. But Adam did not.

By contrast, Jesus, as the husband of the church, interceded. He said, 'Father, forgive them.' And the Father forgave — while we were yet sinners.

## Life Older, Better, Everlasting

The devil used the fear of death to hold people bound in lifelong servitude. But Jesus turned around and destroyed death; through the gospel he brought life and immortality to light.

Fine — let them persecute you. They will misinterpret your intentions. You can suffer for this. You can suffer. But none of that suffering erases the victory already won, or the life already given.

You have life. You don't need to wait to have life — you already have life, that life which is older, better, stronger, and everlasting. It is a life you will never finish.
